Latest research paper used for the research-based simulation

Title: Correct Black-Box Monitors for Distributed Deadlock Detection: Formalisation and Implementation

Authors: Radosław Jan Rowicki, Adrian Francalanza, Alceste Scalas · Year: 2025 · Venue: Proceedings of the ACM on Programming Languages, 9(OOPSLA2), Article 291, pp. 527–554.

Problem: In message-based services, one deadlocked service can cause other services to wait, hiding the core deadlock and making diagnosis difficult.

Approach: Deploy a monitor beside each service. Monitors observe incoming and outgoing messages and exchange probes. The authors formalise their model and report soundness and completeness for the supported model.

Implementation: The authors implemented DDMon for monitoring Erlang/OTP applications and evaluated its performance.

Application demonstrated here: A simplified dependency graph shows how a wait cycle can be surfaced. It illustrates the paper's motivation and core concept, but does not reproduce DDMon or its formal proof.

Limitations: This is a teaching simulation using a static directed graph. It does not inspect live network messages, implement probe protocol, or prove soundness/completeness.

Source: DOI: 10.1145/3763069 · Open preprint
